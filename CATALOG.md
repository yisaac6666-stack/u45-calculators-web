## Keyboard numbering, colours & fonts (all 45)

- Every key carries a number badge (1…N) in reading order: sections top→bottom, keys left→right; a navigation pad counts ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ then its centre key. The same numbers are used in the manuals (docs/manuals/). Toggle with “# On/Off” (remembered).
- Hi-Contrast colour groups (default; “Classic” restores model colours): white/black **digits**, yellow/black **operators & execute**, blue/white **functions**, green/black **memory & variables**, red/white **clear/delete/power**, black/cyan **shift, modes, menus & navigation**.
- Three bundled fonts (offline, CSP-safe): **U45 Display** (Oxanium) for displays/headings, **U45 Keys** (Barlow Condensed) for key legends, **U45 Mono** (JetBrains Mono) for key numbers and helper text. Licences: fonts/LICENSE-FONTS.txt (SIL OFL 1.1).
