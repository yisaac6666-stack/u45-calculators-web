About U-45 Calculators

U-45 Calculators is a free online calculator collection that runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up. It brings together 45 working calculators in one place: a scientific calculator online with natural textbook display, a simple school calculator for everyday homework, a full graphing calculator for plotting lines, parabolas and trig curves, a fraction calculator and percentage calculator for quick sums, a financial calculator for loans, mortgages, savings and time value of money, and a classic RPN calculator with a four-level stack.

It is designed as a calculator for students and for exam practice: each calculator opens with a worked example so you can see exactly which keys to press, and the key layouts follow familiar natural-display, one-line, graphing and business calculator styles. You can use the keyboard or tap the on-screen keys, switch between degrees and radians, work with fractions, powers, roots, logarithms, statistics, complex numbers, matrices and number bases.

U-45 Calculators works offline. After your first visit everything is stored on your device, and you can install it as an app (PWA) on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS, Linux or Chromebook. It is free to use, has no ads, and keeps your calculations private because everything runs locally in your browser.

Whether you need to check homework, solve a quadratic, graph a function, work out a percentage discount, add fractions, calculate a loan or mortgage payment, or practise RPN stack arithmetic, U-45 Calculators gives you the right tool in a couple of taps. Choose one of the five categories – Scientific, Pocket, Graphing, Financial or Reverse Polish Notation – or browse the full catalogue of all 45 calculator models below.

Frequently asked questions

Is U-45 Calculators free?

Yes. U-45 Calculators is a free online calculator with no ads, no sign-up and no download required. Open the page in any modern browser and start calculating.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the whole app is cached on your device, so the scientific, graphing and financial calculators keep working without an internet connection.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. U-45 Calculators is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Use your browser's "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" option to install it on a phone, tablet or computer.

Is it a good calculator for students and exams?

It includes school and exam-style scientific calculators with natural textbook display, fractions, percentages, statistics and trigonometry, so students can practise with familiar key layouts. Always check your exam's rules about which physical calculators are allowed.

Which types of calculators are included?

45 calculators: natural-display scientific, pocket, one-line, RPN, programmer, matrix, statistics, graphing (monochrome, color, CAS, 3-D), financial (TVM, loans, mortgages) and printing desk calculators.

Is my data private?

Yes. Calculations run locally in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
