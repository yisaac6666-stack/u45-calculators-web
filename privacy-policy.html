Privacy Policy — U-45 CALCULATORS

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App version 1.5.2 · Package com.calc45.app Developer: yisaac6666-stack (Uganda) · Contact: yisaac6666@gmail.com

Summary

U-45 CALCULATORS does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device only

  • Passcode: your 4–8 digit PIN is stored only as a one-way SHA-256 hash.
  • Settings and preferences (sound, layout, theme), in app local storage.
  • Calculation history and notes: encrypted on the device with AES-GCM.
  • "Social" feature: profiles, posts, groups and messages are kept in a local database on your device only. There is no server, and nothing is uploaded or seen by other people unless you share it yourself.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. We cannot access or recover it.

Camera

The camera permission is used only by "Camera Solve" to photograph a math problem, which is recognised on the device. Images are not stored or uploaded. The camera is optional, and you can deny the permission.

Network connections

  • Currency converter: when you use it, the app requests public exchange rates from open.er-api.com or api.frankfurter.app / api.frankfurter.dev over HTTPS. No personal data is sent, but these services receive your IP address and standard request headers, as with any internet request. See their own privacy policies.
  • Optional OCR language packs: if you turn this on (it is off by default), language files are downloaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
  • Sharing: if you tap a share button, the content you choose is passed to the app or website you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). That service's own policy then applies.

The app blocks all other connections, and it does not use unencrypted (HTTP) traffic.

Security

The app uses a screen-capture block, an app passcode, encrypted storage and an integrity check to protect your data. On rooted or jailbroken devices it shows a warning, because other apps may be able to read app data; the check runs on the device and nothing is reported.

Health calculators

The health and fitness calculators give estimates for information only. They are not medical advice, and no health data leaves your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
